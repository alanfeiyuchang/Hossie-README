# Hossie — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-09_

This Privacy Policy describes how the Hossie iOS app ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

## Information the App Stores On Your Device

The App stores the following on your device only:

- **Display name** you choose for play.
- **Profile avatar image** if you select one from your Photo Library.
- **Language preference** (English or Simplified Chinese).
- **Game preferences and progress** (such as match counters and the most recent settings).
- **Redeem codes** you enter to unlock seasonal themes.

This information is kept in standard iOS storage (UserDefaults and the app's sandbox). It is not transmitted to us.

## Information Shared With Other Players During Multiplayer

When you join a multiplayer room, the following information is shared **only** with the other players in that same room, for the purpose of running the game:

- Your chosen display name and avatar image.
- Your in‑game actions (bids, plays, mode choices, Quick Chat selections).
- Connection metadata required to keep the room synchronized.

## Third‑Party Services

- **Apple MultipeerConnectivity** is used for offline (LAN / Bluetooth) multiplayer. Discovery happens only on your local network.
- **Photon Realtime** by Exit Games is used for online multiplayer. When you join an online room, game traffic is routed through Photon's servers. Photon receives the data needed to run the room (display name, avatar thumbnail, room state). Please refer to [Photon's Privacy Policy](https://www.photonengine.com/legal/privacy) for their practices.

We do not use third‑party advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or attribution providers.

## Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not require an account, email, or phone number.
- We do not collect contacts, precise location, microphone, browsing history, or device identifiers for tracking.
- We do not sell any data.

## Permissions

- **Photo Library**: optional, used only when you pick a profile avatar. Photos remain on your device.
- **Local Network / Bluetooth**: optional, used only to discover nearby players for LAN multiplayer.
- **Camera**: only requested if you use a "take a photo" avatar option.

You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings → Hossie.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change.
